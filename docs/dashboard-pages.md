# Dashboard Pages

## Executive Dashboard

Purpose: provide a high-level view of overall e-commerce performance.

KPIs: - Total Sales - Total Profit - Total Orders - Total Quantity -
Average Order Value - Profit Margin %

Charts: - Sales trend - Profit trend - Sales by region - Sales by
product - Sales by category

Filters: - Region - Segment - Category - Order Date

## Product Performance Dashboard

Purpose: understand which products, categories, and sub-categories
contribute to sales and profit.

KPIs: - Total Products - Total Categories - Total Sub-Categories -
Average Sales per Product

Charts/table: - Category sales - Sub-category sales - Sub-category
profit - Product sales - Product profit - Product-level detail table

## Customer & Regional Analysis Dashboard

Purpose: analyze customer and geographic performance.

Charts/table: - Customer sales - Regional sales - State-level sales
map - Segment sales - Person/sales-representative sales - Customer sales
and profit table

Filters: - Region - Segment - Category - Order Date

## Returns & Business Insights Dashboard

Purpose: identify patterns in returned orders and connect them to
business actions.

Charts: - Returns by category - Returns by region - Returns by product -
Returns over time

Filters: - Category - Region - Segment - Order Date

The page also contains a written business-insights panel summarizing the
main observations from the dashboard.
