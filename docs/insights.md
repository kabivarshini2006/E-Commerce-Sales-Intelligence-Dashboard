# Dashboard Insights

The following observations are documented directly in the Returns &
Business Insights page of the Power BI report:

-   The Consumer segment generated the highest share of total sales.
-   Technology achieved the highest revenue among the categories.
-   The West region recorded the highest number of returned orders.
-   A few products contributed disproportionately to total returns.
-   Monitoring return patterns can help reduce losses and improve
    customer satisfaction.

These statements are dashboard observations based on the report's data
and visualizations. They should be interpreted within the filters and
time period represented by the Power BI model.
